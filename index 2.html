The Mission

Every child deserves to learn these things before they need them.

Destiny Beyond the Numbers exists to help young people understand their worth, recognize healthy and unhealthy boundaries, identify safe adults and safe relationships, use their voice, and know where to turn when something does not feel right.

We believe prevention begins with knowledge.

Our goal is to create conversations that help young people feel informed, empowered, heard, and worthy of safety.

My Story

I Know What It Means to Survive.

My story began with experiences I never should have had to navigate as a child.

But my story did not end there.

Through foster care, adoption, loss, healing, motherhood, and finding my voice, I began to understand something:

What happened to us can become part of our story without becoming the definition of our future.

Today, I share my story to help create safer conversations for the next generation.
